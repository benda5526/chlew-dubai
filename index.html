The Chlew story

Born in Paris, raised on great bread and even better charcuterie — Chlew is a love letter to the French deli. Now we're bringing it to Jumeirah Beach.

Every sandwich is built to order with our own oignon bread, slow-cooked meats and house sauces. No shortcuts, no fillers — just real, generous flavour, wrapped up and ready to go.

Whether it's a quick lunch by the beach or a full charcuterie board for the crew, on s'occupe de tout.
